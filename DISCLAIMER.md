# Disclaimer

The Indonesia OSINT & PII Protection Framework is provided for cybersecurity education, prevention, and responsible data-protection practices. It is intended to help users understand Indonesian personal-data categories, prioritize safeguards, and assess potential exposures within an authorized scope.

IPCR (data criticality) and ERS (exposure risk) are proposed project models. They are not official Indonesian government classifications, certifications, legal opinions, or substitutes for an organization's approved risk methodology, professional security assessment, or legal/privacy advice. Scores are decision aids and must be supported by evidence and context.

Use this material only for lawful, authorized, privacy-conscious work. Do not use it to identify, profile, track, harass, dox, or target private individuals; obtain data without authorization; bypass access controls; or acquire, store, or distribute leaked databases and credentials.

Do not commit real NIK/KTP values or images, KK numbers, NPWP values, bank details, health records, identity documents, passwords, session tokens, API keys, private keys, or other sensitive personal information to this repository. Use clearly fictional examples or appropriately anonymized descriptions.

Users are responsible for determining the legal, contractual, sector-specific, and organizational requirements applicable to their work, including Indonesia's personal-data protection requirements. If an exposure is suspected or confirmed, follow the organization's incident-response process and obtain appropriate security, privacy, and legal guidance.

**Protect personal data. Respect privacy. Use OSINT ethically.**
